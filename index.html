Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where exactly is Eden Brook Estate?

In Arepo, off the Lagos–Ibadan Expressway, entering through the Arepo community main entrance. It's about 12 minutes from Berger and roughly 10 minutes' drive from the Lagos State Secretariat, Alausa. Book an inspection and we'll take you there.

Why is the price ₦65M when plots around it sell for more?

This is a pre-launch price. Early subscriptions raise the funds to start filling the estate's road network. After pre-launch, the price moves to ₦100M per plot.

How does the instalment plan work?

Pay an initial deposit of ₦15M, then spread the balance over up to 24 months, paying at least ₦2.1M a month. The exact schedule is set out in your offer letter.

What is the outright price?

Pay for your plot at once and get a 10% discount on the ₦65M pre-launch price.

Who builds the roads?

The layout is new. Way and Life Concepts will fill it to give allottees road access after allocation.

Can I buy more than one plot?

Yes. Use the calculator above to see the cost of multiple plots, then tell us in the form.

I live outside Nigeria. Can I still buy?

Yes. Fill the form and our team will arrange a video inspection and walk you through the documents and payment process remotely.

How do I know my payment is safe?

Our team will give you the official company payment details after your inspection. Always confirm them by calling 0816 299 9735 before paying, and never pay into a personal account. You'll receive a receipt for every payment.
